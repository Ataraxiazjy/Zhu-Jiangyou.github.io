About Me


Zhu Jiangyou received the M.S. degree with the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing, China. She is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree with the Department of Information Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong, under the supervision of Professor Chen He. Her research interests include radar sensing and its applications in robotic and AI systems.
